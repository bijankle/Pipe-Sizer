Pipe Sizer

How it works

Procedure

  1. Pick a material and pressure rating for each pipe column.
  2. Fill any two of flow rate, velocity and nominal diameter.
  3. Tap an output badge to choose which of the three is solved — the third is computed automatically.
  4. Use + Add pipe to compare several sizings side by side; the × on a column removes it.

General notes

Flow, velocity and diameter are linked by Q = v · (π/4) · ID². Enter any two and the third is computed; tap an output badge to choose which field is solved. Diameter results use the internal diameter for the chosen size and rating.

Steel ID = OD − 2 × wall thickness (ASME B36.10M). PE100 uses the mean bore from AS/NZS 4130; the PN rating maps to the SDR series internally (e.g. PN10 = SDR17, PN16 = SDR11). When solving for diameter it rounds up to the next discrete size whose bore meets the required area, so the actual velocity stays at or below your target.

Velocity dot: green ≈ 1–3 m/s (typical carrying range), amber outside it.
